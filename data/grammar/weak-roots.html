Weak roots גְּזָרוֹת

"Weak" roots contain a letter that can't follow the normal pattern, so the verb bends. They're not exceptions to memorize one by one — each type bends the same way in every binyan. Many of the most common verbs are weak, so this page pays off fast.

1. Roots ending in ה — ל״ה

The ה isn't really pronounced. Present ends in -e / -a; past switches to -iti; future ends in -e.

VerbPresent (m/f)Past: I / he / sheFuture: I / he
לִקְנוֹתbuyקוֹנֶה / קוֹנָהקָנִיתִי / קָנָה / קָנְתָהאֶקְנֶה / יִקְנֶה
לִרְאוֹתseeרוֹאֶה / רוֹאָהרָאִיתִי / רָאָה / רָאֲתָהאֶרְאֶה / יִרְאֶה
לַעֲשׂוֹתdoעוֹשֶׂה / עוֹשָׂהעָשִׂיתִי / עָשָׂה / עָשְׂתָהאֶעֱשֶׂה / יַעֲשֶׂה
לְחַכּוֹתwait (pi'el)מְחַכֶּה / מְחַכָּהחִיכִּיתִי / חִיכָּה / חִיכְּתָהאֲחַכֶּה / יְחַכֶּה

Infinitives end in ־וֹת: לִקְנוֹת, לִשְׁתּוֹת, לְנַסּוֹת. "She" in the past ends in ־תָה.

2. Hollow roots (ו or י in the middle) — ע״ו / ע״י

The middle letter disappears in most forms, leaving a short one-syllable verb.

VerbPresent (m/f)Past: I / heFuture: I / he
לָגוּרliveגָּר / גָּרָהגַּרְתִּי / גָּראָגוּר / יָגוּר
לָבוֹאcomeבָּא / בָּאָהבָּאתִי / בָּאאָבוֹא / יָבוֹא
לָקוּםget upקָם / קָמָהקַמְתִּי / קָםאָקוּם / יָקוּם
לָשִׂיםputשָׂם / שָׂמָהשַׂמְתִּי / שָׂםאָשִׂים / יָשִׂים

Careful: for these verbs, present "he" and past "he" sound the same (הוּא גָּר — "he lives" / "he lived"). Context decides.

3. Roots starting with נ — פ״נ

The נ drops out when it would come right after a prefix — in the future and infinitive — and the next letter doubles.

VerbPresentPast: IFuture: I / he
לִנְסוֹעַtravelנוֹסֵעַנָסַעְתִּיאֶסַּע / יִסַּע
לָתֵתgiveנוֹתֵןנָתַתִּיאֶתֵּן / יִתֵּן
לִיפּוֹלfallנוֹפֵלנָפַלְתִּיאֶפּוֹל / יִפּוֹל

Commands are very short: תֵּן! (give!), סַע! (drive!) — though people still prefer תִּיסַּע.

4. Roots starting with י — פ״י

The י shows in the present (יוֹשֵׁב) and past (יָשַׁבְתִּי) but disappears in the future and infinitive, which get an e sound.

VerbPresentPast: IFuture: I / he
לָשֶׁבֶתsitיוֹשֵׁביָשַׁבְתִּיאֵשֵׁב / יֵשֵׁב
לָצֵאתgo outיוֹצֵאיָצָאתִיאֵצֵא / יֵצֵא
לָרֶדֶתgo downיוֹרֵדיָרַדְתִּיאֵרֵד / יֵרֵד
לָדַעַתknowיוֹדֵעַיָדַעְתִּיאֵדַע / יֵדַע

In hif'il these roots show a ו instead: לְהוֹרִיד (take down), לְהוֹצִיא (take out), לְהוֹדִיעַ (announce).

5. Throaty letters — א ה ח ע ר

These "guttural" letters dislike a plain shva and can't be doubled, so nearby vowels shift:

  • An a instead of o in the pa'al future: יִשְׁמַע, יִשְׁלַח
  • A chataf vowel instead of shva: אָכַלְתִּי but אֲכַלְתֶּם, לַעֲבוֹד
  • A longer vowel instead of doubling: סֵירֵב (pi'el, middle ר), לְהֵירָדֵם (nif'al)

The good news: you hear the right vowels in speech, and the spelling of the letters hardly changes.

How to use this page

Don't learn the tables — learn the verbs, and come back here when one surprises you. Each new weak verb will then "click" into a type you already know.
