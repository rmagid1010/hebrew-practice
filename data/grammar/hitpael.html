Hitpa'el בִּנְיַן הִתְפַּעֵל

Hitpa'el is the reflexive and reciprocal binyan: doing something to yourself (get dressed, shower), to each other (correspond, see each other), or a change of state (get excited, get used to). Like pi'el, it doubles the middle root letter.

The shape

With the root ל־ב־שׁ (wear):

  • Present: mit–a–e → מִתְלַבֵּשׁ (mitlabesh)
  • Past: hit–a–e → הִתְלַבֵּשׁ (hitlabesh)
  • Future: yit–a–e → יִתְלַבֵּשׁ (yitlabesh)
  • Infinitive: lehit–a–e → לְהִתְלַבֵּשׁ (lehitlabesh)

The ת right before the root is the giveaway. In past and future the vowels are identical — only the prefix changes (הִתְ־ vs יִתְ־ / תִּתְ־ / אֶתְ־).

Meanings

  • To yourself: לְהִתְלַבֵּשׁ (get dressed), לְהִתְקַלֵּחַ (shower), לְהִתְאַמֵּן (work out)
  • To each other: לְהִתְרָאוֹת (see each other → נִתְרָאֶה!), לְהִתְכַּתֵּב (text back and forth), לְהִתְנַשֵּׁק (kiss)
  • Becoming / feeling: לְהִתְרַגֵּשׁ (get excited), לְהִתְבַּיֵּישׁ (be shy), לְהִתְרַגֵּל (get used to), לְהִתְעַצְבֵּן (get annoyed)
  • Just a verb: לְהִתְקַשֵּׁר (call), לְהִתְחַתֵּן (marry), לְהִתְלוֹנֵן (complain)

The ת swap

When the root starts with a hissing sound, the ת jumps after it, and with צ / ז it even changes:

Middle letter can't double?

With א ה ח ע ר in the middle, the vowel shifts a little: לְהִתְרָאוֹת, לְהִתְנַהֵג (behave). Same binyan, same prefixes.
