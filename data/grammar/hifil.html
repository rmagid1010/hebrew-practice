Hif'il בִּנְיַן הִפְעִיל

Hif'il is the causative binyan — making something happen — and it's full of high-frequency verbs: start, feel, understand, order, explain, bring, remember, recommend, arrive.

The shape

With the root ז־מ־ן (time / invite):

  • Present: ma–i → מַזְמִין (mazmin)
  • Past: hi–i → הִזְמִין (hizmin); with endings the i becomes a: הִזְמַנְתִּי (hizmanti)
  • Future: ya–i → יַזְמִין (yazmin)
  • Infinitive: leha–i → לְהַזְמִין (lehazmin)

The long i (written with י) before the last root letter is hif'il's fingerprint.

Plenty of hif'il verbs are just everyday verbs with no "causing" feel: הִתְחִיל (started), הִרְגִּישׁ (felt), הִסְבִּיר (explained), הִמְלִיץ (recommended).

On the street

Commands are the future: תַּסְבִּיר לִי (explain to me), תָּבִיא לִי (bring me), תַּזְמִין לִי (order for me). And everyone ends calls with אֲנִי מַגִּיעַ — "I'm on my way".
